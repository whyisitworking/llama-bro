One Models screen

The final design of D116, drawn in the app's own tokens: light and dark side by side, each phone at 412 × 915 dp shown at 62%. It replaces Get models and the library. Fit is a symbol, never a word on screen; its words are the symbol's content description and long-press tooltip. Rows carry no description line.

The screen: groups

Search, storage, then Downloading, On this phone and Suggested for this phone. A suggestion that is already on the phone or downloading is not repeated. A row has the name with its fit symbol, the maker, then capability icons and the download count. Tapping any row opens its card.

Search: the loading slot and badges

A thin bar runs in a fixed slot under the field while a search is under way; the slot is always there, so nothing moves. The previous list stays until the answer comes. A result carries the repository's state: a percentage with a thin bar, On phone, Queued or Paused. The embedding model shows the no-entry symbol and a muted name.

The card: downloading

The state comes from the repository, so a download started anywhere shows here. No Get: the bar, bytes and time left, Pause and Cancel. The header carries the capability icons with their words, which is where the icons are learned.

The card: on this phone

"Chat with" opens a new chat on this model. "Get another size" reveals the size choice, with the installed file marked and not selectable.

The card: not here

The size choice, with each size's fit as a symbol, and Get pinned at the foot while the rest scrolls. Can't-chat models show a line in Get's place.

The card, pulled up: the model's page

Pulled up, the card shows the Hugging Face page of the original model as markdown, under the header and above the pinned action. In the build the card opens at most 560 dp tall and goes full height once its content is scrolled up; images in the page are dropped and HTML tables flatten to text.

The fit symbols

One 20 dp box and one weight. Four are Material Symbols (sprint, directions_run, elderly, block); the kneeling figure is drawn for the app, tired and not hurt. Inks: great, plain ink, slow, and the quiet ink for too big and can't chat.
