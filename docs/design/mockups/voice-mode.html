Voice mode

A spoken conversation with a model that talks (D95, D100), drawn in the app's own tokens: light and dark side by side, each phone drawn at 412 × 915 dp and shown at 62%. Nothing here is built yet; it is for review.

Getting there: the message bar

For a model that speaks, an empty field puts the voice button in Send's slot, filled with the send indigo: the one strong action when there is nothing to send. The mic for a voice message (D76) moves just before it. Typing turns the slot back into Send.

Ready

The orb is still. One line says what to do; the talk button is the only control. The top bar names the model and the voice; ✕ returns to the chat.

Listening: holding the button

The button fills with the send indigo while held. The orb swells with the voice's level and a hairline ring spreads from it, so the person sees they're heard. Releasing sends the turn.

Thinking: the moment before the reply

About 0.3 s on the phone (F170). The orb's light turns once around it; the person's words appear above, captioned (D99).

Speaking, with captions

The orb moves with the reply's speech. The caption grows under the person's words, in large sans; older lines fade at the top. Stop ends the reply; holding talk interrupts it and listens.

Back in the chat

Each turn is an ordinary message: the person's as a voice bubble with its caption under it, the reply as its text, with a speaker action that replays its speech. The chat goes on by typing.
